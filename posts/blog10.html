I Don’t Want a Relationship But I Still Feel Lonely

Published on April 3, 2026

I don’t really know what my problem is but I will just say what I feel.

I feel attracted to girls, that is one part. But I don’t talk to them. That is another part. And even if I think about it, I don’t want a relationship. I don’t see myself going out, dressing up, trying to impress someone. That is not me.

I might text random people sometimes, mostly because I am bored or feeling a little lonely. But that is different. There is no responsibility there. I can leave anytime I want.

A real relationship doesn’t work like that. It needs effort, time, consistency. And honestly, I don’t think I can sustain that. Not because I can’t, but because I don’t want that kind of life.

Why do I still feel lonely?

This is the confusing part.

Even though I don’t want all that, I still feel lonely sometimes. Especially when I am doing nothing, or at night, or when my mind is just empty.

Then I feel like talking to someone. That’s when I open some chat app or message someone. It helps for some time. Then again the same feeling comes back.

Lonely → chat → feel okay → stop → lonely again

It feels like a loop.

Maybe it’s not about needing someone

I started thinking maybe it’s not that I need a partner.

Maybe it’s just that my mind doesn’t like being empty. When I am busy or with my family, I feel completely fine.

But when there is nothing going on, my mind starts searching for something. That feeling, I call loneliness.

Another thing I noticed

I also have this habit of staring at girls if I find them attractive. I don’t act on it, I don’t talk, but I just notice it and keep looking sometimes.

I don’t know why I do that. Maybe it’s just attraction with no place to go.

What I understood

I don’t think I am built for relationships. I don’t want that effort, that lifestyle. I feel better staying at home, with my parents and siblings.

But at the same time, I have normal feelings like attraction and sometimes loneliness.

So maybe the problem is not the feeling.

Maybe the problem is that I try to fix it every time.

“I feel like this right now. It will pass.”

I think if I can reach a point where this feeling doesn’t control me, then I will be fine.
