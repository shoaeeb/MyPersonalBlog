Cutting Off My Younger Sister Completely

Published on October 24, 2023

Me and my younger sister don’t have a good relationship. For the past 2–3 years, we haven’t really talked at all, and I have finally reached a point where I need to be honest with myself about why.

The Weight of Constant Disrespect

She is always rude to me. She keeps saying things like I am dirty, that I don’t wash my hands, and small things like that. It feels like she is always trying to belittle me. I feel like she is very selfish and mostly thinks about herself. She doesn’t try to understand me, and whenever she talks, it feels like she is always right. The way she speaks makes it sound like she knows everything and no one else does.

"Yes, she is better than me in studies. That is true. But that doesn’t give her the right to disrespect me."

Choosing Peace Over Obligation

During this Eid, I didn’t go out with her. Not because I was busy or full, but simply because I didn’t want to. I think I have reached a point where I don’t want to force anything anymore. She has already blocked me on all platforms—WhatsApp, Telegram. Honestly, I don’t care about that anymore. I don’t even know which college she goes to, and I don’t feel the need to know.

I am writing this just to make my mind clear. I don’t want to keep thinking about this again and again. I don’t want to expect anything from her. Whatever relationship we had is already gone, and I am accepting that. From now on, I just want to stay in my own space and not let this affect me anymore.
