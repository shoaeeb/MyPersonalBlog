I Deleted My LinkedIn Account

Published on April 6, 2026

I deleted my LinkedIn account. I don’t feel like using that app anyway. It just reminds me of everything I failed at.

I feel like I failed in my career. I don’t think I really have any chance to get a job. Even learning new skills doesn’t feel like it would change anything anymore.

Feeling Left Behind

People I studied with in college are doing fine now. They have years of experience, jobs, and stability. And I’m still here, sitting at home, doing nothing.

It feels like I’m stuck while everyone else moved forward.

No Motivation Left

My parents tell me to study for government exams. But I just can’t find the motivation to study anymore. I really hate studying now.

They keep telling me to do something, anything. And I am trying in my own way — applying again and again — but there is no result.

"Sometimes it’s not about being lazy. It’s about feeling like nothing you do will change anything."

I Feel Done

I don’t really have friends to talk to. Most days, it’s just me and my thoughts.

At this point, I just feel like I’m done. Maybe these things are just not for me.

I don’t know what comes next.
