So Battery Is Dead

Published on April 9, 2026

One or two months ago, my cat Kookie gave birth to five kittens. I named one of them "Battery" because she looked like a tiny little battery.

She was full of energy—always playing, drinking milk, and sleeping peacefully. She was very cute.

Three days ago, she suddenly became sick. She stopped eating and drinking and just sat quietly on the floor. Today, she passed away.

It happened so simply, like something that happens every day, but it didn’t feel ordinary at all.

No kittens seem to survive in our house. In the last five months, eight cats have died, and six of them were kittens.

I hope Battery, wherever she is, is doing well. I will always love her.

In Islam, it is said that when animals die, they become dust of Paradise. I hope she is there, happy and playing with other kittens.
