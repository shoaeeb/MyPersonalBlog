A Mental “Circuit Breaker” Moment

Published on April 30, 2026

I have been dealing with depression and a lot of repetitive thoughts. These thoughts tend to loop again and again, and it becomes hard to step out of them.

Today something simple happened. I saw a girl I had seen before in my apartment building. For a brief moment, my thoughts stopped. It felt like a sudden break in the loop.

The “Circuit Breaker” Idea

The best way I can describe it is by comparing it to a circuit breaker in an electrical system. When a wire gets overloaded, the circuit breaker cuts off the power to prevent damage. In the same way, this moment interrupted the overload in my mind.

“It wasn’t about the person. It was about the sudden break in my thoughts.”

I am not attaching any meaning to the person I saw. I am understanding it as a mental effect. My attention shifted, and the loop of thoughts stopped for a short time.

What I Learned

This showed me that my thoughts can be interrupted. Even if it happens randomly, it means the loop is not permanent. It can break.

Now the focus is to understand this better and find ways to create these small breaks on my own, instead of depending on random moments.
