Lily and Pilu

Published on March 6, 2026

I am writing this just two days after my two kittens, Lily and Pilu, passed away. Their deaths were sudden, and it still feels difficult to process. They were small parts of my everyday life, quietly present, and now the house feels different without them.Lily last few days used to follow me around and Pilu used to sit on my laptop keyboard.

Over the years, many of the cats that have come into my home have not lived very long. Some stayed for a few years, some for only months, and some for just a few days. Each time it happens, it leaves a small emptiness. Even when you try not to become too attached, it is impossible not to care about them.

Lily and Pilu were gentle and innocent creatures. They did not understand the world the way humans do. They simply lived, played, and trusted the people around them. Remembering that makes their absence feel even heavier.

I really wish this is not the end. If I make it to Jannah one day, I hope I can meet them again.

That is all I wanted to write for now. Wherever they are, I hope Lily and Pilu and all the other cats that came to my life are doing well.
