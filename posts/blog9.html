The Struggle with the Imam's Voice

Published on March 7, 2026

Am I sinful if I say the way the Imam recites the Quran when he leads the prayer is terrible?

He recites like he is singing a song, and sometimes he recites so loudly in the middle of the prayer that it feels like I am sinning just because I don't like it. I honestly don't feel like actually listening to his voice when he leads the congregation.

"I know if I am sinful I am not gonna repent about this because at the end of the day when I die I might be in Jahannam."

I just really wish I make it to Jannat, otherwise I am cooked.
